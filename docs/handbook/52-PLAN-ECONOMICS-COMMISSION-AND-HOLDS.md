# Plan Economics, Commission and Payout Holds

## Purpose

This chapter is the owner-confirmed economic contract for marketplace plans. It defines semantics, not final price numbers.

## 1. Baseline marketplace economics

- Baseline NEXUS marketplace commission: **10%**.
- The 10% is calculated from the amount actually paid after seller/platform discounts, subject to provider/tax accounting rules.
- For now the baseline applies to every seller.
- Future Creator subscriptions may reduce the percentage.
- Exact reduced rates are **OPEN** and must not be invented in production billing logic.

A lower commission is a subscription benefit, not a trust badge and not a hidden negotiated fee.

## 2. Seller payout hold

Initial policy:

- new seller baseline hold: **7 days**;
- subscription may shorten hold;
- verification may shorten hold;
- healthy sale/refund/dispute/fraud history may shorten hold;
- target lower bands may reach roughly 1–3 days;
- exact algorithm/table remains OPEN.

No subscription can override fraud, chargeback, dispute, compliance or provider-mandated reserve/hold conditions.

## 3. Creator Free

`Creator Free` is a true free commercial tier:

- create seller-capable profile;
- create product drafts;
- submit/publish paid resources;
- manual moderation queue;
- baseline marketplace commission;
- baseline payout hold;
- standard analytics/support surfaces.

The free tier must remain usable enough that selling does not require a subscription.

## 4. Paid Creator capability family

Paid Creator capabilities may include:

- automatic/manual-queue-bypass publishing when risk policy allows;
- advanced seller analytics;
- automation;
- richer promotion tools;
- higher safe quotas;
- shorter eligible payout hold;
- future reduced marketplace commission;
- other operational advantages that do not buy verification or moderation favoritism.

Mandatory malware/integrity/policy/risk checks always remain.

## 5. One-off seller monetization

A user without Creator subscription may pay for specific one-off services, especially:

- accelerated publication processing;
- automation for one publication/release where safe;
- resource promotion/featured placement;
- fixed-term advertising.

One-off payment cannot buy:

- a Verified badge;
- guaranteed moderation approval;
- security-check bypass;
- dispute advantage;
- SCAM/blacklist removal.

## 6. Server paid family

Server monetization may combine:

- multiple plan tiers, e.g. Basic/Pro/Business naming to be decided later;
- one-time boosts/featured placements;
- analytics/history/customization/team tooling;
- promotion allowances;
- operational features.

Exact tier names/prices/quotas are OPEN.

## 7. Organization Pro

Organization billing is primarily **per organization**, not seat-metered by default.

Organization plans may govern organization-owned resources, servers, team/admin features, billing/finance tooling, audit and collaboration.

Seat caps may still exist as quotas, but the owner decision is that the commercial unit is the organization.

## 8. NEXUS Pro

`NEXUS Pro` is the maximal cross-ecosystem bundle rather than only a personal cosmetic subscription. It can aggregate benefits from:

- Creator;
- Server;
- personal/profile features;
- Organization capabilities.

Exact allocation rules are OPEN, e.g. how many servers or organizations one bundle can cover. Do not infer unlimited entitlements.

## 9. Global pricing policy

Base subscription prices are globally consistent.

The UI may:

- display approximate local currency;
- settle in provider-supported currencies;
- show taxes/provider fees where applicable.

The initial target is not a hidden country-by-country regional price matrix.

## 10. Transparency

`/pricing`, checkout and seller financial UX should clearly expose:

- current commission;
- hold duration/status;
- what can reduce commission/hold;
- subscription period;
- recurring vs one-time charge;
- provider/tax differences when known;
- cancellation/expiry effects.

No paid plan may imply that Trust, verification, moderation outcomes or disputes can be purchased.

## 11. OPEN numerical decisions

Still require owner/business approval:

- exact Creator tiers and monthly/weekly/yearly prices;
- exact Server tiers and prices;
- Organization price;
- NEXUS Pro price;
- commission reduction table;
- payout hold reduction table;
- quotas/storage/team limits;
- one-off publication and promotion prices.
